Free Bridge for Odoo 19

Sales Commission – Point of Sale

Somebody who sells over the counter in the morning and on invoice in the afternoon should be paid for both.

A bridge, not an app

There is nothing here to open and nothing to configure. Install Sales Commission and Point of Sale and this appears by itself. A bridge you have to remember to install is not really a bridge.

What it adds

Same plans, same rules

Till sales are matched against the rates you already set, per product or per category, and land on the same commission record as that person's invoiced business. One period, one figure, one conversation.

Refunds subtract

A till refund carries negative quantities of its own, so it comes straight off the commission. Money handed back is not money earned.

Off per plan, if you want

Include POS Sales sits on the plan. Clear it and that plan goes back to covering invoiced business only, while other plans carry on counting the till.

Traceable to the ticket

Each line keeps the till order it came from, next to the invoice lines, so a queried figure can be traced to the exact ticket.

One decision worth knowing about

A plan's Earned On setting - confirmed order, posted invoice, or paid invoice - is ignored for till sales. An order at a till is rung up, paid and settled in one motion, so all three describe the same instant and there is nothing to tell apart. Till sales are counted whichever basis the plan uses.
