Sales Incentives for Odoo 19

Sales Commission

Work out what your salespeople have earned, from the documents you already have, without a spreadsheet nobody else can check.

A plan says three things

Build it once, assign it to as many salespeople as you like.

Earned on A confirmed sales order, a posted invoice, or only once the customer has actually paid. Pick the one your business argues about least.
Calculated on Revenue or margin. On margin, a salesperson who discounts their way to a loss earns nothing for it - which is usually the point of putting a plan in place at all.
At what rate Per product, per product category, or one rate for everything. The first matching rule wins, so you put the exceptions above the general case and the general case becomes the fallback.

What that looks like

A plan with 10% on one product, 5% on a category, and 2% on everything else.

Document Base Rate Commission
Invoice - the 10% product 200.00 10% 20.00
Invoice - something in the 5% category 100.00 5% 5.00
Credit note - part of it came back -100.00 10% -10.00
Total 200.00 15.00

Credit notes subtract. A refunded sale does not stay commissioned.

Auditable by construction

Every commission keeps a line per source document line: the document, the customer, the product, the quantity, the base it was calculated on, the rate that applied and the resulting amount. When a salesperson queries a figure you show them where it came from, instead of recomputing it and hoping you get the same answer twice.

Draft, confirmed, paid

Recompute as often as you like

Running a period again replaces its draft rather than stacking a second copy on top. Late invoices and corrections are picked up simply by computing again.

Confirmed figures stay put

Once confirmed or paid, a commission is never quietly rewritten by a later recompute. What you agreed with someone stays what you agreed with them.

Selling at a till as well?

Install Sales Commission - Point of Sale, a free companion that appears by itself once you have both this and Point of Sale. Till sales then count towards commission using these same plans and rules, on the same commission record - so somebody who sells over the counter in the morning and on invoice in the afternoon is paid for both.

Till refunds subtract, and you can switch it off per plan.

See it working

A plan is three rules and two settings. Earned on a posted invoice, calculated on revenue, and the rates ordered so the exceptions sit above the general case - one product at 10%, a category at 5%, everything else at 2%. The first match wins, which is why the order is draggable.

Every line, and where it came from. Invoices, a credit note at -100.00 taking 10.00 back, and till orders all on one record - each with the quantity, the base it was calculated on, the rate that applied and the resulting amount. Nothing here has to be recomputed to be explained.

Draft, confirmed, paid. Once it is agreed it stops moving - a later recompute leaves it alone.

Three menu items, no more. The records, the compute, and the plans behind them.

What this app does not do

Worth knowing before you buy.

It does not pay anybody

Marking a commission as paid records that you paid it. It does not post a journal entry or feed a payslip. Those vary far too much between countries to guess at.

One plan per salesperson

A person has one plan at a time, with as many rules inside it as you need. Splitting one person across several plans at once is not supported.

No rule builder beyond product and category

Rates are set per product, per category, or across the board. Conditions on the customer, the sales team or the order size are not available.

Requirements
