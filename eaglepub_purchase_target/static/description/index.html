Procurement Targets for Odoo 19

Purchase Targets

Set what you intend to buy from each vendor - and measure it on what actually turned up, not what somebody ordered.

Ordered is not received

A purchase order is a statement of intent. Goods that never arrived, arrived late, or came back as a return are not procurement you should be credited with. Achievement here is read from done stock moves on confirmed purchase orders, so a target reflects what is actually on your shelves.

What it does

Per vendor, per product Targets are set against a specific vendor and the products you actually buy from them, rather than one blanket number for the department.
Amount or quantity Measure in money or in units. For commodities where the price moves week to week, quantity is usually the honest measure.
Counted when it arrives Receipts are attributed to the period the goods arrived in, not the period the order was raised. A December order landing in January counts for January.
Returns net off Goods sent back to the vendor come off the achievement. Stock that did not stay is not stock you received.
Periods that keep themselves Monthly, quarterly, yearly or custom, with dates computed for you - and auto-renew into the next period so nobody has to remember on the first of the month.
What is still to come Each line carries current stock, ordered but not received and to replenish alongside the target, so the same screen answers "how are we doing" and "what do I still need to raise".
Reported three ways View on screen, print to PDF or export to Excel, filtered by vendor and product over a quarter, a year or a range you choose.

See it working

One target, and the whole picture. Vendor and period at the top, then a line per product carrying target quantity, cost, current stock, what is on order but not yet in, what is still to replenish, and what has actually been received - in quantity, in money and as a percentage.

Next month in one click. The breadcrumb tells the story: August is done, September exists, and nobody re-keyed a vendor, a product list or a set of dates to get there.

Reporting, with the boundary spelled out. Pick a quarter, a year or a custom range, narrow it to particular vendors or products, then view it, print it or take it to Excel. The dialog states plainly that only targets falling entirely inside the range are counted, so a half-covered period never quietly skews a total.
