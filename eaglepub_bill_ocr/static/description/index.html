Bill Digitisation for Odoo 19

Vendor Bill OCR & Digitisation

Drop in a PDF or a photograph of a supplier invoice and get a draft bill back - vendor, dates, lines, taxes and totals.

Bring your own key

Reading is done by a vision model you configure and pay for directly - Claude or OpenAI. There is no per-page fee to this app, no credit bundle to top up, and no middleman holding your documents.

It also ships with a sample reader that needs no key at all, so you can install it, press the button and watch the whole flow before deciding whether to pay anyone for anything.

Is this for you?

The answer is different depending on which Odoo you run, so here is both.

On Community

There is nothing else

Odoo Community has no bill digitisation at all. Every supplier invoice is typed in by hand, line by line, by somebody who could be doing something else.

Digitisation is an Enterprise feature, and this is how you get it without changing your licence. If you are typing bills today, that is the entire argument.

On Enterprise

Only for three specific reasons

You already have digitisation, and this does not replace it for the sake of it. It is worth buying if one of these is true:

  • Your documents must not leave your control. Odoo's extraction posts them to its own service. Here they go to a provider you choose, under your own contract.
  • You process enough pages for credits to matter. There is no per-page charge from this app - you pay your model provider directly.
  • You want the two checks Odoo's does not make - see below.

If none of those apply, the built-in extraction is fine and you should keep using it.

Three things it refuses to do

In accounting, a plausible wrong number is worse than an obvious blank.

It checks its own arithmetic

Extracted lines are re-added and compared to the totals printed on the page. A reader that misplaces one digit still produces a perfectly believable bill - only the sum gives it away, so a bill that does not add up is flagged rather than filled in quietly.

One of the two checks Enterprise's own extraction does not make.

It never posts anything

Every result lands as a draft for a person to check. The raw extraction is kept on the record, so when a figure is queried you can show what was read off the page instead of re-reading it and hoping for the same answer.

It will not invent a vendor

Vendors are matched on VAT number first, then on name. One that cannot be matched is left empty for you to pick - creating a contact from a misread name quietly litters the address book, and choosing the right one takes seconds.

The details that decide whether it is usable

Numbers as they are written 1,234.56 and 1.234,56 both read as the same amount, and (12.00) is understood as minus twelve - the bracket notation that turns a credit into a charge if you miss it.
Dates that are not ambiguous Several written formats are accepted, and anything that cannot be read with confidence is left blank rather than guessed at.
Taxes only when they match A rate read off the document is matched to a purchase tax in your company. If there is no match, no tax is applied - an absent tax is visible to whoever checks the bill, a wrong one is not.
One at a time, or a pile Digitise a bill you are already looking at, or drop a batch of documents into the upload screen and get one draft per file. A document that cannot be read still produces its bill, flagged, with the file attached - dropping it would leave you working out which invoice never arrived.
Refused before it costs you Files that are not a PDF or a photograph are rejected up front, rather than sent, paid for, and rejected at the far end.

Working against purchase orders?

Install Vendor Bill OCR - Purchase Orders, a free companion that appears by itself once you also have Purchase. When an order reference is printed on the invoice the bill is linked to it, and any difference between what was ordered and what has been billed is flagged - which is the whole reason to raise a purchase order in the first place.

The second of the two checks Enterprise's own extraction does not make.

What this app does not do

Worth knowing before you buy.

Your documents go to your provider

Reading happens at Anthropic or OpenAI, whichever you configure. Supplier invoices are commercial documents, so check that sending them to that provider is something your data protection policy allows. Nothing is sent until you enter a key, and the sample reader sends nothing at all.

It reads, it does not decide

No approval workflow, no automatic posting, no payment. It fills a draft and stops. Everything after that is Odoo's own accounting, unchanged.

Products are not matched

Lines are created with the description printed on the invoice rather than matched to products in your catalogue, because a wrong product silently moves stock. Matching by supplier code is a candidate for a later version.

Requirements

Odoo version 19.0 - Community and Enterprise
Depends on Invoicing - standard, nothing else
You will need An API key from Anthropic or OpenAI, billed to you by them. Or nothing at all, to try the sample reader.
Outbound access Your Odoo server must be able to reach the provider's API.
