Sales Targets for Odoo 19

Sales Targets

Revenue targets for a salesperson on the products that actually matter - not one number for everything they sell.

A number per person is rarely the useful number

Most teams do not want "sell more". They want a particular line pushed this quarter, by a particular person. Targets here attach to a salesperson and a product, so the goal you set is the goal you actually meant.

What it does

Targets that track themselves

Achievement is read from confirmed sales orders as they come in, with a progress bar on the target itself. Nobody maintains a spreadsheet alongside.

How much is left to sell

A computed quantity to reach target turns a percentage into something a salesperson can act on this afternoon.

Periods that keep themselves

Monthly, quarterly, yearly or custom, with the dates computed for you, so setting next quarter is not a data-entry exercise.

Everyone sees their own

Salespeople see their targets and nobody else's. Managers see the team. Targets are a motivator, not a league table you did not agree to.

A person or a whole team

Assign a target to an individual salesperson or to a sales team, and measure it on amount, quantity or both - because a target of "sell 200 units" is not the same conversation as "bill 200 thousand".

Reported three ways

View on screen, print to PDF or export to Excel, over a quarter, a year or a range you pick, filtered by salesperson, team or product.

See it working

One target, and the whole picture. Assign it to a salesperson or a whole team, pick the period, then a line per product carrying the target, the sales price, current stock, and what has been achieved - in quantity, in money, as a percentage, and as the quantity still to go.

Reporting, with the edges spelled out. A quarter, a year or a custom range, filtered by salesperson, team, product or measure, then viewed, printed or taken to Excel. The dialog says plainly that only targets falling entirely inside the range are counted, and that choosing both salespeople and teams widens the result rather than narrowing it - the two are alternatives, not a combined filter.

Requirements

Odoo version 19.0 - Community and Enterprise
Depends on Sales and Inventory - both standard
Pairs with Purchase Targets, the same idea on the buying side
