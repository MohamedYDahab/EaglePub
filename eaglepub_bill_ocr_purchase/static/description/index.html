Free Bridge for Odoo 19

Vendor Bill OCR – Purchase Orders

A supplier billing more than they quoted is exactly what a purchase order exists to catch. This is the app that notices.

A bridge, not an app

Nothing to open and nothing to configure. Install Vendor Bill OCR and Purchase and this appears by itself. It exists so the OCR app does not have to drag Purchase in for everybody who only wants to digitise bills.

What it adds

It flags the difference

The billed total is compared against the ordered total and any gap is written on the bill in words: "Billed 90.00 against P00001 at 60.00 - a difference of 30.00." Noticed on the bill, not at payment time.

Untidy references still match

Suppliers quote your order number however they like - "Ref PO00012", "our order po-00012". The digits are matched rather than the whole string, so a tidy reference is not required.

A weak reference is no reference

Two or three stray digits will not link an order. Guessing wrong is far worse than leaving the bill unlinked, because a bill silently tied to somebody else's order is a mistake nobody goes looking for.

It reports, it does not correct

Nothing is adjusted to make the two agree. Which figure is wrong - the order or the invoice - is a judgement only a person can make, and often a conversation with the supplier.
