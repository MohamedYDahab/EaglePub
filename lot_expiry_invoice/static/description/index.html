Overview

This module displays Lot/Serial Numbers and Expiry Dates on invoice lines. The information is automatically fetched from related stock moves (from sales orders or purchase orders).

Key Features

  • Display lot/serial numbers on customer invoice lines
  • Display lot/serial numbers on vendor bill lines
  • Show expiry dates for tracked products
  • Automatically links stock move lots to invoice lines
  • Adds lot and expiry columns to invoice PDF reports
  • Works with both sales and purchase workflows
  • Optional columns - show/hide as needed

How It Works

When you create an invoice from a sales order or a bill from a purchase order, the module automatically detects the lot/serial numbers used in the related stock picking operations.

If the product has expiration date tracking enabled, the expiry dates are also displayed for each lot.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18.0
  • Products must have lot/serial tracking enabled
  • For expiry dates: Product Expiry module must be installed
  • Invoice must be created from a sale order or purchase order with completed deliveries

Configuration

No special configuration required. After installation:

  1. Open any customer invoice or vendor bill
  2. Click on the column selector (⋮) in the invoice lines
  3. Enable "Lot/Serial" and "Expiry Date" columns

Technical Information

Model Extended: account.move.line
New Computed Fields:
- lot_ids (Many2many to stock.lot)
- lot_names_display (Char)
- expiry_dates_display (Char)
- has_lot_tracking (Boolean)
