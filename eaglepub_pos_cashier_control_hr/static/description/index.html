Free Bridge for Odoo 19

POS Cashier Control – Employee Cashiers

Policies should follow the person who served the customer, not whoever happened to open the till that morning.

Why this exists

With badge login, the Odoo user behind a session is whoever opened the till - frequently a supervisor who never touched the sale. The person who actually served the customer is an employee, and a policy attached to an Odoo user never reaches them.

That failure is silent. The limits look configured on screen and quietly enforce nothing, which is the worst way for a control to fail.

What it adds

A policy on the employee

POS Policy appears in the employee's HR settings. Set it there and every limit - discount ceiling, order ceiling, line deletion, price changes - applies to that person whichever till they badge into.

Falls back, does not replace

An employee with no policy of their own uses the one on their linked Odoo user. Set it in whichever place suits how you already organise people, and nothing has to be entered twice.

Enforced in both places

The till and the server both resolve the policy from the employee, so an order cannot be edited past it in transit any more than it could before.

A bridge, not an app

Nothing to open and nothing to configure beyond the field itself. Have POS Cashier Control and Employees in the Point of Sale, and this installs on its own. A correctness fix nobody remembers to install is not a fix.

Requirements

Odoo version 19.0 - Community and Enterprise
Depends on POS Cashier Control and Employees in the Point of Sale. Installs itself once both are present.
